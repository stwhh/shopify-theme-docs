Scroll animations

Scroll animations

Sheer includes built-in scroll-triggered entrance animations that add an editorial, premium feel to your storefront. No coding required.

How it works

As customers scroll down the page:

  1. Section headings (eyebrow text, title, description) fade up into view
  2. Product cards and image grids appear one after another in a staggered ripple — each card follows the previous one by the stagger delay set in Animation settings

The effect is subtle and professional — similar to what you see on Awwwards-winning brand websites.

Controlling animations

Go to Theme settings → Animation to:

  • Turn all animations off — uncheck "Enable scroll animations"
  • Adjust stagger delay — how quickly cards follow each other (0–200ms)
  • Change speed — slow / medium / fast

Animations only play once when the element first enters the viewport. Scrolling back up and down won't re-trigger them.

Loading skeletons

When a section has no content configured yet (for example, a product card section without a selected collection), it displays skeleton placeholders — gray cards with a shimmering light sweep. This gives a polished, app-like loading feel instead of blank space.

The skeletons are automatically replaced with real content once you configure the section in the theme editor.

Hover effects

Product cards

On desktop, hovering over a product card triggers:

  • Image zoom — the product image gently scales up (4%)
  • Shadow lift — the card gains depth with a soft shadow
  • Quick-add reveal — if "Show quick-add on hover" is enabled in Animation settings, the Add to cart button slides into view

Buttons

All primary buttons have a subtle lift and glow effect on hover, reinforcing the clickable area.

Accessibility

All animations are automatically disabled for customers who have Reduce motion turned on in their operating system. The theme also provides a global Enable scroll animations toggle in the theme editor for merchants who prefer a static layout.
