Contact us

Contact us

Use the form below for theme support. We typically reply within 1–2 business days (Mon–Fri).

What to include

  1. Shopify store URL (e.g. your-store.myshopify.com)
  2. Sheer theme version — Theme settings → Theme information
  3. Expected vs. actual behavior
  4. Steps to reproduce
  5. Screenshots or screen recording (attach above — up to 25 MB per submission on Forminit Pro)
  6. Custom code or apps in use

Do not send your Shopify admin password. We will never ask for it.

Email alternative

If the form fails, email stwhh572@gmail.com with subject [Sheer Theme] and the details above.

What we can help with

In scope Out of scope
Sheer sections and theme settings Custom development beyond theme scope
Theme bugs Shopify billing or account issues
Filters, markets, navigation setup Heavily modified code from other developers

For Shopify platform questions, use the Shopify Help Center.
