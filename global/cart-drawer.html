Cart drawer

Cart drawer

Sheer uses a right-side cart drawer so shoppers rarely need the full /cart page.

What opens the drawer

  • Product page Add to cart
  • Featured product Add to cart
  • Product card Quick add (single variant)
  • Product recommendations quick add

Drawer contents

  • Line items with image, title, variant, price
  • Quantity +/- controls (compact stepper)
  • Remove line (trash icon, same as the cart page)
  • Subtotal
  • Checkout button
  • Optional Cart note (enable in Cart drawer section settings)

Merchant notes

  • Do not install apps that force redirect to /cart unless intentional.
  • Test checkout from the drawer after changing payment or shipping settings.

Add to cart goes to cart page instead of drawer?

Usually a third-party app or custom script is overriding the cart. Disable recent apps one at a time to test, or mention installed apps when contacting support.
